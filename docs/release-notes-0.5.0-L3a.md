# Quiet 0.5.0 L3a — the reader bar (native test build)

The first half of layer 3 (`docs/plan-native.md`). Installs over L2.1, next to 0.4.2.

## What's new
- **The tuning scale:** your place as a needle on a scale, each chapter a station mark. Tap or
  drag it to move (it clicks as you pass a chapter). Under it: the page (`177 / 571`) and the
  time left in the book.
- **Five keys:** Contents · Day/Night · Light · Text · More. **Customize bar** (in More, or
  Settings → Reader): hold ≡ and drag to reorder; tap a key below to swap it in. Extra keys:
  Layout, Go to, Scroll/Pages.
- **More:** theme, Light, Text, Look & layout, Contents, Go to page, Customize bar (Bookmarks,
  Notes, Search and Book info show which layer brings them).
- **Light:** brightness (follow the phone, or Quiet's own) and warm light.
- **Look & layout:** line spacing, margins, alignment, weight, paragraph spacing, first-line
  indent, hyphenation, and "back to the book's own layout".
- **Go to page** with −10/−1/+1/+10, and **Back to page N** after any jump (Contents, Go to, the scale).
- **Volume keys** turn pages (down = next). **Time and battery** in the corner while reading.
  **Keep the screen on** while a book is open. All three in Settings → Reader.
- These settings go into backups (and come from a 0.4.2 backup).

## Check on your phone
1. Open a book: the scale, page and time left show at the bottom. Drag the needle: does it jump, and does "Back to page N" bring you back?
2. Light: brightness changes the screen; warmth tints the page amber.
3. Look & layout: line spacing, margins, justify, hyphenation, indent: does the page follow?
4. Customize bar: drag a key to a new place; swap in "Go to". Does the bar change?
5. Volume keys turn pages (in Pages mode and in Scroll mode).
6. The clock and battery show in the top corner when the bars are hidden.
7. The screen stays on while reading.
