# Quiet 0.5.0 L8.1 — fixes from the phone

Installs over L8, next to 0.4.2.

## Fixed
- **Curl:** it folds only the reading area, copied exactly. Books that paint their own white page
  (with margins, like a white card) now show Quiet's page colour, so the whole page is one sheet.
  This also stops a white box under Night text.
- **Vibration:** every tap now clicks the same way: menu rows, More rows, notes, colour dots, search
  results, Undo, and switches. On phones that don't clearly support Android's built-in "click"
  (many Xiaomi phones), Quiet plays a short pulse of its own instead of a faint or missing one.
- **Switches:** the switch keeps its margin inside the card. Tap anywhere on the row.
- **What's new:** the whole story, from the first HTML file to now.
- Reader tips: a new test runs the real app from the first start (tour → Skip → open a book) and
  the tips appear. If they don't on your phone: About → The tour, then open a book.

## Check on your phone
1. Pages + Curl on Barbarians at the Gate: no white box; the fold is the page.
2. Night with that book: no white box.
3. Tap around (Settings rows, More, Notes, a highlight colour, switches): each one clicks.
4. Settings → Reader → Bionic reading: the switch sits inside the card.
5. About → The tour, then open a book: the reader tips.
6. About → What's new: scroll to "The first Quiet".
