# Quiet 0.3.0

The third version of Quiet: more comfortable reading, a tidier library, notes and quotes from every book in one place, translation, and reading stats.

Install **Quiet-0.3.0.apk** over any earlier version. Your books, places, bookmarks and highlights stay.

## Reading comfort
- **Themes:** System (Day or Night, following your phone), Day, Sepia, Night, **Black** for OLED screens, and **Custom** colours.
- **Text:** weight, space between paragraphs, first-line indent. Word breaks keep whole words by default, with hyphenation as an option.
- **Warm light**, a **reading ruler** (line or focus), and **bold word starts** (bionic reading).
- **Clock and battery** in the corner while the controls are hidden.
- **Footnotes** open in a small window. **Pictures** open full screen with pinch and double-tap zoom.
- **Go to** any percent, and a **Back** chip after links and jumps.
- **PDFs and comics:** pinch to zoom, plus zoom buttons.
- **Android:** screen rotation lock and light vibration.

## Library
- **Tags**, **star ratings** and a **short review**.
- **Want to read** status.
- **Edit more details**: series, language, description. **Change the cover.**
- **Sort** by series, rating or file size, and **group by author**.
- **Shelf view**, with pinch to resize.
- **Select several books** at once.
- **Recently deleted**: removed books stay 30 days, with Undo.
- **Find duplicates.**

## Notes and quotes
- **Underline** and **strikethrough** besides highlighting.
- Save passages as **quotes**.
- **Tags on highlights.**
- A **Notes** screen with highlights, notes and quotes from every book: searchable, by book or newest first, and it opens the book at the passage.

## Translate
- Select text and **translate it in a small window**, with meanings for single words.
- Translation uses Google Translate, so the selected text is sent to Google. Nothing else leaves your phone.

## Reading stats
- **Time read** today, this week and this year, **pages** and **reading speed**.
- A gentle **streak**, a **daily goal** and a **yearly book goal**.
- "**You read for 25 min**" when you close a book.

## Also
- A short **welcome** for new readers, and **What's new** after updates.
- **Accessibility:** names for every button, clearer grey text, a keyboard focus ring, and less motion when your phone asks for it.
- The **Contents** button is next to the chapter name, and an **app text size** setting.
