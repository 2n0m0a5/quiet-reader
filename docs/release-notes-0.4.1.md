# Quiet 0.4.1 — Stats, like a radio

## New
- **The Stats tab in the Radio look.**
  - **Today** is a needle meter: from zero to your daily goal (or to an hour, without one), with
    the stretch past the goal in orange. The needle swings up when the tab opens.
  - **This week** is seven columns of level lights; today's are orange.
  - **Three readouts:** days in a row, words a minute, books this year (against your yearly goal).
  - **Everything else**, your reading year, when you read, your pace, finished books, book by
    book, totals and the library, is behind one key: "Year, hours, pace and every book".

## Check on your phone
1. Stats: does the needle swing up to today's minutes? Set a daily goal and look again.
2. The week's lights: the day you read most is a full column; today is orange.
3. "Year, hours, pace and every book" opens the rest below.
