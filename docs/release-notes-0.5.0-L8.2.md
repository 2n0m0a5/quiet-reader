# Quiet 0.5.0 L8.2 — motion

Installs over L8.1, next to 0.4.2. The owner asked for iOS-like motion everywhere, and for the
TalkBack work and the reader tips to go.

## New: motion
- **Books open out of their cover.** Tap a cover: the book grows from it into the full page, the
  cover fading into the first page. Close it and it shrinks back into the same cover (which may
  have moved to the front of "recently read"). Opened from Notes, it rises and fades in.
- **The back swipe follows your finger** (Android 14 and later): an open book, or a Settings page,
  slides off to the right as you swipe, with the library or the previous page waiting underneath,
  dimmed less and less. Let go early and it springs back; let go further and it finishes leaving.
- **Settings pages slide in like iPhone:** the new page comes in whole over the old one, which
  drifts a third of the way left and dims; back is the same in reverse.
- **Scroll mode, chapter to chapter:** at a chapter's end the page stretches with resistance as you
  keep pulling (iOS's rubber band). A quiet line says "Pull for the next chapter", then "Release
  for the next chapter" with a click. Let go: this chapter glides up and away and the next rises
  in. Let go early: the page springs back. The same at a chapter's start, for the previous one.
- **Tabs glide** a little toward the tab you chose, and its icon dips and springs back.
- **Tap the tab you're on** to glide back to the top (Library, Notes, Stats).
- **Choices slide:** the raised key glides to the option you tap, and its orange dot pops in.
- **Covers sink under your finger** and spring back; the select tick pops in.
- **The library reflows:** books glide to their new places when you sort, filter, group or
  remove them; notes too.
- **The Day/Night key turns** as the moon and sun swap. **Messages** spring up from the bottom.
- **Stats numbers count up** when the tab opens. The reader's bars and the selection bar slide.
- Motion → Reduced (or the phone's "Remove animations") makes all of it instant.

## Removed
- The TalkBack additions from L8 (headings, read-out charts, the scale as a slider).
- The reader tips (the app tour stays, in About).

## Check on your phone
1. Tap a cover: does the book grow out of it? Back key: does it shrink back into the cover?
2. In a book, swipe back slowly from the edge: the page follows your finger. Let go early (springs
   back), then swipe further (closes).
3. Settings → Interface → back swipe, slowly. Tap Interface, Reader, About: the slide.
4. A book in Scroll mode: at a chapter's end keep pulling up; read the line, feel the click, let go.
5. Tap the tabs; tap Library again while scrolled down.
6. Theme: tap Day/Night/Custom (the key slides). The moon key at the top.
7. Library: sort or filter (books glide), hold a cover (tick pops, bar slides up).
8. Motion → Reduced: everything instant.
