# Quiet 0.4.0 — Radio

A new look, controls at your thumb, and a library that can't get lost.

## New
- **One design: Radio.** After a 1960s radio: warm grey, keys that press down, a dotted speaker
  grille, numbers like a readout, and colour only where it means something (orange: you are here;
  green: finished). Day and Night. New fonts (Archivo, DM Mono, Crimson Pro) and a new icon: a
  volume knob turned all the way down. The four old styles are gone.
- **The reader bar.** At the bottom of the page: a scale with a tick for each chapter, and
  Contents · Day/Night · Light · Text · More. More has every other control. Choose your own four
  keys (More → Customize bar).
- **Library backup and restore.** One `.quietbackup` file with everything: books, places, notes,
  quotes, reading time, fonts and settings. Restore it on this phone or another; nothing is deleted.
- **Safety copy.** The Android app keeps a second copy of your library and brings it back by itself
  if the phone loses Quiet's data (this happened once and emptied a library).
- **Book info.** Length in words and pages, reading time at your pace, chapters, ISBN, what's in
  the file, and your own reading: time, sittings, highlights, notes, quotes.
- **Settings in sections:** Interface, Reader, Library, Files & storage, Privacy & translation, and
  About Quiet (version, what's new, the tour, your data, promises).
- **The tour:** tips on the real screens for new readers (and from About).
- **Report a problem:** a short report you read first and send yourself, by email or Telegram.
- **Motion setting:** Follow phone, Full or Reduced. It says when your phone asks for less motion
  (Battery saver can).
- **Stats** is the name of the old Reading tab; it opens only from its tab now.

## Fixed
- Text that a book coloured too faintly (black on Night, white on Day) or faded out is readable;
  the chapter that looked empty while its links worked.
- The page curl: it bends like paper now, uses the page's own colour, curls only the page of a
  PDF, and never shows both pages at once.
- Curl needs page mode; choosing it in scroll mode switches to pages.
- Book folders added nothing after the library was lost ("no new books").
- Clicks right after opening a book (the reading settings button on desktop) were lost.
- The mouse wheel didn't scroll What's new and other dialogs on desktop.
- "Saved" after saving a quote picture was hidden behind the dialog.
- A long press on a book could open its details and close them at once.
- Sepia is gone (it becomes Day).

## Check on your phone
1. Install over 0.3.4: your library should still be there (and is copied into the safety copy).
2. Settings → Files & storage → Back up: save the file; Restore it (nothing doubles).
3. Read a book: the bar at the bottom, Day/Night, Light, Text, More → Customize bar.
4. Pages mode + Curl: the curl, on an EPUB and on a PDF, in Day and Night.
5. Settings → Interface → Motion: what does the hint say?
6. Hold a cover → the book info.
7. About → Take the tour; Report a problem → Telegram.
8. The new icon on the home screen.
