# Quiet 0.5.0 L4 — notes (native test build)

Layer 4 (`docs/plan-native.md`). Installs over L3b.1, next to 0.4.2.

## What's new
- **Select text** (hold a word, drag the handles): **Highlight · Note · Quote · Translate**, then
  the phone's own Copy and Share.
- **Highlights** in four colours (yellow, green, blue, pink) and four looks: highlight,
  underline, strikethrough, plain. Tap a highlight to change it, add a note, make it a quote,
  add tags, share it as a picture, copy or delete it. A new highlight takes the last colour you used.
- **Quotes** are kept apart, with who says them (empty means the author).
- **Quote pictures:** "Picture" draws the quote on a page in Radio's colours and opens Share.
- **Bookmarks:** the key at the top right of the reader. Orange when this page has one.
- **In the reader:** More → Notes, quotes & bookmarks (filter by kind; tap to go there, with
  "Back to page N"), and More → **Search in book**. Both can also be keys on the bar
  (Customize bar: Notes, Search).
- **The Notes tab:** everything from every book, newest first, with search and filters. Tap to
  open the book at that spot; hold to edit (a bookmark: hold to remove).
- **Translate:** into the language in Settings → Privacy & translation (or pick in the sheet).
  Only the text you translate is sent (Google, or MyMemory if Google refuses).
- **Notes from 0.4.2** (in a backup) show in the Notes tab and are drawn in the book (Readium finds the text in its chapter).
- PDFs: bookmarks work; highlights and search work in EPUB and TXT.

Note: highlights you make here travel in backups, but 0.4.2 can't draw them on its pages (it
lists them in its Notes tab). That's fine, since the native app replaces 0.4.2 at the switch.

## Check on your phone
1. Hold a word, drag the handles, tap Highlight: does it stay coloured? Tap it: does the editor open?
2. Note, Quote (with a speaker), Translate (with the internet on).
3. Strike and Underline: do they look right on the page?
4. Bookmark key: orange on this page, off on the next; the list in More → Notes, quotes & bookmarks.
5. Search a word in the book; tap a result.
6. The Notes tab: tap a note → the book opens there. Hold → the editor.
7. Picture on a quote → Share opens with the picture.
8. Restore your 0.4.2 backup: are your old highlights there, in the right places?
