# Quiet 0.5.0 L8 — polish (native test build)

Layer 8 (`docs/plan-native.md`) without the switch: the app ID stays `app.quietreader.n`, so it
installs over L7, next to 0.4.2 (which stays).

## What's new
- **The tour:** 6–7 short tips that point at the real buttons (Add, your books, ⋯, Notes, Stats,
  Day/Night, backups). It runs once on the first start (and once now, after this update). Tap
  anywhere or Next; Skip or Back ends it. Replay it from Settings → About → The tour.
- **Reader tips:** the first time you open a book, 7 tips over the reader (turning pages, the
  scale, Contents, Text, Light, hold a word, More). Replaying the tour replays these too.
- **What's new:** shown once after each update; also in About.
- **Report a problem:** Settings → Report a problem (or About → Report). You see the whole report
  first (version, phone, settings, counts, recent errors, the last crash; no books or notes),
  add what happened, then send it by Email or Telegram, or copy it. Quiet now keeps its last crash.
- **About:** your data (books, space, last backup, highlights, notes, quotes, fonts) and every
  library Quiet uses, with its licence.
- **Custom theme:** Settings → Interface → Theme → Custom: choose the page and text colours, with
  a sample, and a warning if the two are hard to read together. Backups carry them (web app too).
- **Motion:** Reduced now covers everything: no slides, no springs, page turns without a slide,
  curl becomes a plain turn. Follow phone does the same while the phone's "Remove animations" is on.
- **Text size (80–150%):** keys, tabs and numbers stay on one line at large sizes (they shrink a
  little instead of breaking words).
- **TalkBack:** titles are headings (swipe by headings), choices say "selected", every switch row
  is one switch you can tap anywhere on, messages are read as they appear, the scale under the
  book is a slider (swipe up/down to move), Stats days and charts are read out, selected books and
  notes say "selected".

## Check on your phone
1. Install over L7. The tour starts: step through it, then try Skip/Back on a replay (About → The tour).
2. Open a book: the reader tips show once. Close and reopen: they don't come back.
3. Settings → Report a problem: read the report, type a line, try Telegram and Copy.
4. About: the numbers look right for your library; What's new opens.
5. Interface → Theme → Custom: pick a dark page and light text; open a book in it.
6. Motion → Reduced: Settings pages, tabs and page turns don't slide. Then back to Follow phone.
7. Text size 150%: tabs, About and the reader bar still read well.
8. If you can: TalkBack on (volume up + down for 3 s, if set up), move around Settings and the reader bar.
