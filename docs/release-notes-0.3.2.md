# Quiet 0.3.2

Install **Quiet-0.3.2.apk** over any earlier version. Your library, notes and settings stay.

## Two design styles
Choose how Quiet looks in **Settings → Style**. Switching is instant, and nothing else changes.

- **Quiet Star** (the new default): almost nothing, and one small star. Source Serif and Figtree type, lapis blue on soft paper, round buttons and segmented controls. The eight-pointed star is the logo, the bookmark, the rating and the slider handle.
- **Paper**: black ink on white paper, nothing else. Newsreader and Libre Franklin type, no boxes, just hairlines and white space. Words instead of buttons, with the chosen one underlined, and one red dot on the main action. Books are rated 1 to 5, and Notes becomes the *Commonplace book*.

Each style has its own Day and Night colours. Sepia, Black and Custom work in both.

**More styles are on the way**, and later you'll be able to **build your own style**.

## Also new
- **A tab bar**: Library, Notes, Reading and Settings, at the bottom on phones and in the top bar on wider screens. Back still returns to the library.
- Books without a cover get a plain cover that matches the style.
- Sliders show how far along they are.
