# Quiet 0.4.2 — a page you can hold

## New
- **The page curl follows your finger.** In Pages mode with Curl, swipe and keep your finger
  down: the page peels back with its corner under your fingertip, the fold halfway between your
  finger and the edge, like real paper. Let go past about 40% of the way (or with a quick flick)
  and the page turns; let go earlier and it settles back flat on the same page. Taps on the
  edges, the volume keys and the arrow keys still turn with the automatic curl.

## Fixed
- A drag that Android cancels (a notification pulled down, a call) now lets go cleanly instead of
  leaving the page half turned.

## Check on your phone
1. Settings → Reader: Pages and Curl. Open a book.
2. Swipe from the right edge and hold: does the corner follow your finger? Move back and forth.
3. Let go early: the page settles back and you stay on the same page.
4. Drag further (or flick) and let go: the page turns.
5. Swipe from the left edge: the previous page is laid down the same way.
6. Try a PDF, and Night.
