# Quiet 0.5.0 L8.3 — swipe the shelves, Now reading

Installs over L8.2, next to 0.4.2.

## New
- **Swipe between shelves.** In the Library, swipe left or right to move between All, Reading,
  Want, Done and Series. The shelf follows your finger, the orange key in the filter row slides
  with it, and it clicks as you pass from one shelf to the next. Tapping a filter glides there.
- **Now reading.** The book you're in floats just above the tabs, on every tab:
  - **Tap** it (or its book key) to read. The book grows out of the bar's little cover and goes
    back into it when you close.
  - **Swipe sideways** to flip between your recent unfinished books (up to five, dots show which).
  - **Swipe up** to open it; **swipe down** to tuck the bar away (Undo). It comes back after you
    next read.
  - **Hold** for Read, Book page, Mark as finished (Undo), Tuck away, Bar settings.
  - It shows the author, how far you are, and with a daily goal, today's minutes ("12 of 30 min
    today"). A thin orange line along its bottom is your place in the book.
  - It slides away while you scroll down a list and comes back as you scroll up. Lists leave
    room for it at the end, and messages rise above it.
  - **Frosted glass:** what's behind it is blurred, like iOS.
- **Settings → Interface → Now reading:** show it or not, Every tab or Library only, Opacity
  (40–100%: how solid the glass is, live as you slide), and Hide while scrolling.
- The tour has a stop for the bar.

## Check on your phone
1. Library: swipe left and right across the covers; tap a filter too.
2. Now reading: tap it, read, close (back into the bar). Swipe it sideways (if you have 2+ books
   in progress), down (tuck, then Undo), up (opens). Hold it.
3. Scroll the library down and up: the bar slides away and back.
4. Settings → Interface → Now reading: Opacity (watch the bar), Library only, off.
5. Notes, Stats, Settings: nothing hides under the bar at the end of the lists.
