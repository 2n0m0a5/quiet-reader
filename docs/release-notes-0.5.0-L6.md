# Quiet 0.5.0 L6 — stats (native test build)

## 0.5.0 L6.1 (your fixes)
- **Text no longer has Theme:** the Day/Night key is on the bar (System and Black are in More → Theme).
- **One bookmark key:** the one at the top right hides while Bookmark is on the bottom bar.
- **Recently deleted: hold a book to select several**, tap more, then **Put back** or **Delete
  for good** (asks first). **All** selects every book there.
- **Notes tab works the same way:** hold a note to select it (tap opens the book there); then
  **Edit** (one), **Copy**, or **Delete** with Undo. One gesture means one thing everywhere:
  tap opens, hold selects.

Layer 6 (`docs/plan-native.md`). Installs over L5.3, next to 0.4.2.

## What's new
- **Reading time is counted** while a book is open, only while Quiet is on screen and you've
  touched it or turned a page in the last 5 minutes (a phone left on the table doesn't count).
  Pages turned forward count as pages of about 250 words; jumps don't. Saved every 30 seconds.
- **Stats tab, Radio style:**
  - **Today:** a needle meter from 0 to your daily goal (the orange stretch is past it), minutes
    read, and how much is left. **Daily goal:** Off, 10, 20, 30 minutes or 1 hour.
  - **This week:** seven columns of level lights; today's in orange.
  - **Readouts:** days in a row, words a minute (last 30 days), books this year (of your goal).
  - **Books a year** goal, and time read this year and all time.
  - **More stats:** your reading year (26 weeks of days, days read, longest run, best day), when
    you read (hours of the day, weekdays), your pace (12 weeks, average sitting, speed now and
    before), finished books by month (and days to finish one), and book by book (time, days,
    speed, time left).
- **When you close a book:** "You read for 25 min." When you reach your daily goal while
  reading: "Today's goal is done. Well read."
- A book's page now shows its time read, sittings and the day you started.
- Your 0.4.2 reading time comes in with a backup, and goes back out in one.
