# Quiet 0.3.4

Install **Quiet-0.3.4.apk** over any earlier version. Your library, notes and settings stay.

## A fourth style: Frosty Glass
**Settings → Style → Frosty Glass.** Controls of frosted glass float over a clear page, on soft, icy light, following Apple's rules for Liquid Glass:
- Only the floating things are glass: the top bar, a floating tab capsule, toolbars, sheets, menus and toasts. Book pages and lists stay clear.
- Glass **materialises** instead of fading: its blur and light build up as it appears.
- It **swells and lights up** where you press.
- A **liquid lens** slides under the chosen tab and option, stretching like a drop of water as it moves.
- Pulling on to the next chapter shows a drop of glass that fills with light when letting go will turn the page.
- Inter for the app, Literata for books. With Reduce Transparency on, the glass turns solid.

## Everything moves
- **Books zoom out of their covers** when you open them, and back into the same cover when you close them. In Old Tashkent they unfold from the arch; Paper keeps its ink fade.
- **Swipe from the left edge** of a book to go back. The book follows your finger with the library underneath.
- **Icons answer** when tapped: a gear turns, arrows nudge, a bin wiggles, the moon and sun swap through a blur, and the rest bounce.
- **Books glide into place** when you sort, filter, group, change the view, or remove and restore them.
- **Press and hold** a book: it sinks under your finger, then lifts as its details open.
- Numbers on the Reading screen **count up**. Menus on wide screens **grow from where you tapped**.

## Page curl
**Reading settings → Page turn → Curl** (in page mode). Pages turn like paper: lifting at the edge, bending a little and darkening as they turn, with the next page in their shadow. Taps, swipes, arrow keys and volume keys all curl. There's also Slide (as before) and None.

## More kinds of books
- **Books:** Word (**DOCX**), OpenDocument (**ODT**), **RTF** (including Russian files in Windows-1251), **Markdown**, Palm documents (**PDB**, **PRC**), and **HTMLZ** (a web page in a zip).
- **Comics:** **CBR** (RAR), **CB7** (7-Zip), **CBT** (TAR), and plain **zip**, **rar** and **7z** archives of pictures. An EPUB or other book inside an archive opens too.
- Comic pages come in the right order (page 2 before page 10), even with upper-case names like `.JPG`, and junk files from Macs are skipped.
- Opening RAR and 7-Zip needs a small reader (about 1 MB). It's loaded the first time and then kept for offline use.

## Your own fonts
**Settings → Your fonts → Add fonts** (or "Add your own font" in the reading settings).
- Add TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2 files. Quiet reads each font's own name and weight, and puts a family's regular, bold and italic files together as one choice.
- Your fonts appear next to Serif and Sans, each shown in its own letters.
- They stay on this device.

## Series
- A **Series** filter shows each series as a stack of its covers, with how many you've finished.
- Open a series to see its books in order, with a button for the next one to read.
- In the sort menu there's also **Group by series**.

## Share a quote as a picture
- In **Notes**, tap the picture button on a quote or highlight. Quiet draws a card in your style's own colours, type and ornament, with who says it, the book and its cover.
- Choose **Post** (4:5), **Square** or **Story** (9:16), then **Share** or **Save picture**. On Android, saved pictures go to Pictures/Quiet.
